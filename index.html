How the numbers work

Total Revenue
Sum of won-deal revenue within the selected closing dates and filters. Values are in THB.
Total Deals / Clients / Brands
Number of deal rows, unique clients, and unique client–brand pairs. Names are trimmed and matched without case sensitivity.
Year-over-year change
Percentage change against the same date range one year earlier. A dash means there is no comparison data or the previous value is zero.
Service Type
One service type per deal. The inner donut shows revenue across six service types; the outer ring splits each service into its subcategories by revenue. One subtype per standalone deal; missing subtypes remain Unclassified. Influencer subcategories use selling_item. Integration uses its five revenue allocation columns. Unknown services remain visible. Integration allocations must not exceed deal revenue; any remainder is Unallocated. Influencer charts cover Influencer marketing (Only) deals. Tier counts cannot exceed Thai + regional placements; missing classifications are Unknown, distinct from No tier.
Proposing Items
One selling item per deal. Six semicircular tracks compare deal counts against the largest category. The largest fills its track; smaller counts scale proportionally. The table shows each count and its share of all filtered deals. Missing values appear as Unclassified below the table.
Influencer Rate
The donut shows the number and percentage of selected won deals using KOL rate, Fixed rate, or KOL rate & Fixed rate. Each deal belongs to one category. It follows the dashboard filters. Deals without a rate type appear as Unknown, so the chart always totals all selected deals.
Team / BD
Both filters apply to every metric, chart, ranking, export and previous-year comparison. The BD list follows the selected Team. Missing assignments appear as Unassigned. Revenue targets remain unchanged.
Client segments
Each client has one industry, taken from their latest deal. A client is New if any selected deal is marked New, then Existing, otherwise Unknown.
Campaign KOLs
Won deals grouped by their Thai and regional placement counts. Deals with both belong only to Thai & Regional KOLs. Deals with neither appear as Unknown. The dropdown changes only this chart.
Revenue target
Default: THB 100M for a full year, prorated by calendar days for shorter ranges. Edit to set your own target. Remaining cannot fall below zero.
Most Repeat Deals
Total deals per brand, including the first deal. Click a name in any ranking to filter all charts.
